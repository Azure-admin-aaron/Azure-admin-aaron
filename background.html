A career built across the layers.

Support, infrastructure, client consulting, and cloud delivery have each shaped how I think. Together, they help me connect a technical decision to the people and goals behind it.

Start with what happens after go-live.

Support taught me where technical decisions create friction. Infrastructure work broadened that view across identity, networking, endpoints, security, and operations. A solution has to make sense to the people responsible for keeping it running.

Understand the organization, not just the technology.

Consulting meant adapting to different environments, listening to clients, and explaining tradeoffs. It sharpened my judgment about when a design fits—and when a technically sound answer still misses the real need.

Bring those perspectives into Azure.

In my current role, I work across platform engineering, developer needs, identity, and governance. That wider view helps connect business goals with implementation details, and keeps the conversation grounded in what teams need to deliver.
